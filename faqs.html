FAQs

Questions? Answered.

Everything you'd want to know before you apply.

The basics

What is this, exactly?

We pay people to share their honest opinions and experiences in a 45–60 minute conversation. Businesses want to understand how real people think, and we connect them with you. There's nothing to sell and no right or wrong answers.

Why would a company pay for my opinion?

Companies can see what people buy and use, but not why. Talking to real people helps them learn what works, what frustrates customers, and what to improve. That insight is worth paying for.

Do I need any experience or qualification?

No. We're interested in the real experiences you already have as a consumer, a professional, a student or a creator.

Is it free to join?

Yes. You never pay anything to apply or to take part.

Is this a sales call?

No. It's a research conversation about your experiences. Nobody will try to sell you anything.

Earnings and payment

How much can I earn?

Interviews pay between ₹300 and ₹1,500 per call. The exact amount depends on the project, such as the topic and who the company wants to hear from. You're told the payout for a project before you agree to take part.

Is the earnings calculator a promise?

No. It simply multiplies the number of interviews you say you're available for by our lowest and highest payouts. Calls depend on whether a project matches your profile, so it's an estimate of what's possible, not a guarantee.

When and how do I get paid?

You're paid once your interview is completed, as per the terms of that project. We'll confirm the payment method and timing with you before the interview.

Am I guaranteed interviews?

No. We contact you when a project fits your profile. Some people hear from us quickly and others wait longer between projects.

The process

What happens after I submit my details?

We'll call you for a short intro and screening chat. We check that your details are correct and relevant to the projects we run. If all is well, you join our panel and we invite you to paid interviews when a project matches.

Why is there a screening call?

It lets us confirm that your details are real and that you're a good fit for our projects. Entries with incorrect details won't be added to the panel.

How long is each interview?

Usually 45–60 minutes. You choose which projects to accept, so you're never obliged to take one.

Do I have to be on camera?

It depends on the project. If an interview needs video, we tell you in advance.

How do I tell you when I'm free?

On the home page you can say how many interviews you can do each month and whether you prefer weekdays, weekends and which time of day. We try to schedule calls around that.

Privacy and creators

Is my information private?

We use your details only to find research projects that fit you and to contact you about them.

I came through a creator's link. Does that change anything?

No. Your eligibility and pay are the same either way. The creator who shared the link is credited for referring you.
