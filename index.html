Questions

Frequently asked questions

When do I get paid?

Payment is made after the completed interview, according to the research project terms.

How much do interviews pay?

Typical payments are ₹500 to ₹1,500+, depending on the research project and interview length.

How long are interviews?

Most conversations are around 30 to 60 minutes.

Am I guaranteed an interview?

No. Participation depends on whether your profile matches an active research project. We reach out when there is a fit.

How often might I receive opportunities?

This varies based on how many projects match your profile. Some people may hear from us quickly, while others may wait longer between opportunities.

Do I need prior experience?

No. You're being invited because your experience and perspective may be useful, not because of any formal qualification.

Do I need to appear on camera?

It depends on the specific project. If a particular interview requires video, you'll be told before you're asked to join.

What happens after I register?

We may contact you when a project fits your profile and explain the next steps.

Is this a sales call?

No. These are research conversations intended to understand people's experiences and opinions.

Do I have to pay anything to join?

No. Joining Key Stone Intelligence is free.
